# Whole-buffer reads tool/check_whole_buffers.sh finds, one per line:
#   <path under vendor/pdf_oxide>::<fn>#<n>  # table: <why>
# `table` is the only class: a read of something whole by nature, and the why
# names its kind (a font program, a sampled function, an ICC profile, the CMap
# table, a DER certificate or the DSS, the xref stream, one object-stream
# member, a progressive JPEG raster, a JPX recompressed at full size). A read of
# anything else is bounded instead, and the script refuses any other word after
# the `#`.
